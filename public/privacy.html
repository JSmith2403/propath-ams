ProPath Privacy Policy

Operated by Snö Performance Lifestyle Development Consultancy LLC (“Snö Performance”, “we”, “us”), United Arab Emirates. Last updated: 3 November 2026.

ProPath is the athlete management app used by Snö Performance and its coaches. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices you have. It applies to athletes, parents/guardians and coaches who use ProPath.

In short: we use your information only to coach you and look after your wellbeing. We do not sell it. We do not use it to train AI. We never send your Garmin data to AI services. You can see, correct, export or delete your data, and withdraw consent, at any time.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, username or email, and the role on your account (athlete, coach).
  • Coaching records you or your coaches enter: training plans and logs, test results, goals, wellness check-ins, meal and hydration logs and photos, messages, attendance and notes.
  • Garmin data, only if you connect your Garmin account. Depending on what you choose to share in Garmin Connect, this can include sleep, heart rate, stress, body battery, steps, activities and workouts (including GPS routes and heart-rate data from those activities), and — only if you opt in — menstrual cycle and pregnancy tracking data.
  • Technical data: basic device and sign-in information needed to keep your session secure, and a push-notification address if you turn notifications on.

2. How we use it

  • To plan, deliver and adjust your training, and to monitor recovery, workload and wellbeing.
  • To show you your own progress and reports in the app.
  • To let your coaching team support you, for example noticing poor sleep or rising training load.
  • To keep the service secure and working, and to meet our legal obligations.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not use Garmin data for any purpose other than the ones above.

3. Garmin data in particular

  • Connecting Garmin is your choice. You sign in on Garmin’s own page — ProPath never sees your Garmin password.
  • You control which categories of data Garmin shares with ProPath (for example activities, health, women’s health), and you can change or stop this at any time from your Garmin Connect account settings, or by using Disconnect Garmin in the ProPath app.
  • Garmin data is shown to you and to your own coaching team. In ProPath you decide, with simple Yes / No switches, whether your coaches can see your sleep (hours, sleep score and overnight HRV), stress, body battery and women’s health data. Anything you switch to No is hidden from your coaches, and you can still see all of your own data. You can change the switches at any time. Women’s health data is off unless you turn it on; if you do, it is visible only to you and the coaches who work with you directly and is never shown on squad-wide dashboards or reports. Steps, resting heart rate and workouts are shared with your coaches once you connect.
  • When you disconnect, we stop receiving new data. You can ask us to delete the Garmin data we already hold (see section 7).
  • Garmin data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to train, evaluate or improve any AI model.

5. Artificial intelligence

Some ProPath coach tools use AI services, for example to read a photographed training sheet or to help draft a written report for a coach to review. Garmin data is never sent to these services, and no Garmin data is used to train AI. If we ever propose to use Garmin data with an AI system, we will first ask for your explicit consent, explain what it is used for, and give you an easy way to withdraw that consent at any time.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data while you are an active athlete with us. When you leave, or when you ask us to delete it, we delete or anonymise it within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep something longer. Garmin data we have received is deleted if you disconnect and ask us to remove it, or when Garmin tells us your authorisation has ended.

7. Your rights and choices

  • Access: ask for a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correct: ask us to fix anything that is wrong.
  • Delete: ask us to delete your data, including Garmin data.
  • Export: ask for your data in a portable file.
  • Withdraw consent: disconnect Garmin, switch off notifications, or ask us to stop any use you agreed to — at any time, without affecting your coaching.

To use any of these, email us at the address below. We will reply within 30 days. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian can make these requests for you.

8. Security

Each person has their own login. Data is encrypted in transit, access is limited by role so athletes can only see their own records, and public access to the database is switched off. No system is perfectly secure; if we ever become aware of a breach affecting your data, we will tell you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

9. Children

ProPath is used by athletes who may be under 18. Their use is arranged with the knowledge of their parent or guardian, who may contact us about their data at any time.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how we use your information, we will update this page and the date at the top, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
